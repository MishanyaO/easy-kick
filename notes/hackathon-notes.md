
# Challenge 2: Chat Insights / Engagement

Your mission is to answer... How would you turn chat into something more than a scroll of messages?

_Think about:_

- [ ] Would you build for the streamer (insights – what's chat feeling, what's trending, who's showing up) or for the viewer (engagement – polls, predictions, reactions, mini-games)? Either direction is fair game.
- [ ] What would the feedback loop look like - streamer sees something, and viewers see something back?
- [ ] What would you pull from chat to make this work - message volume, sentiment, keywords, emotes, specific commands?
- [ ] How do you keep it simple enough that it adds to the stream instead of getting in the way?

# PITCHING TIPS

- Pick a presenter - 1 person or several (your call)
- Format (5 min pitch, 4 min judges questions, 1 min changeover)
- Start with explaining the challenge _not_ the solution
- You can then present your working demo
- Don't forget to cover off the judging criteria
- Practice/practice/practice (make sure that it is < 5 mins)
- Have fun with it!

# SUGGESTED '6 STEP' FRAMEWORK

1. Hi, we're team xx..
2. The challenge we are solving is xx ..
3. Our possible solution is xx..
4. This is a big opportunity because... xx
5. Execution - who is the target user & what are the next steps to progress?
6. Summarise & thank you

# A GREAT PITCH...

- Often includes a real-life story or example that brings the challenge to life
- Is clear, concise and doesn't include jargon that may not be easily understandable to the audience
- Articulates why the problem is worth solving and the benefit if the problem is solved
- Excites the audience! A great presentation demonstrates passion for the problem / opportunity that the team is tackling
